### Commercium Without Common Ground: Why Interaction Does Not Require a Common Origin

- **Author:** Daniel Molinero Lucas
- **Type:** Preprint
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Published:** 2026-09-25
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22968837
- **Zenodo:** https://zenodo.org/records/22968837
- **Derived from:** Exergism commit `56732a0d8d8f149f9339a2dc47bee14b1d962325`

The paper formalizes a Common-Ground Principle drawn from Kant's 1770
*Inaugural Dissertation* and rejects it with finite countermodels in which
members of distinct lineages genuinely interact without sharing a unified
origin. It argues that commerce requires a shared form or ontological space,
not a common genesis.
